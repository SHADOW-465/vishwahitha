# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- **Chennai Youth & Prospective Members:** High-potential college students and young professionals seeking to develop leadership skills and run community service projects.
- **Corporate Sponsors & Partners:** Corporate CSR divisions, public bodies, and community trust groups looking to fund or co-execute community relief campaigns.
- **Active Club Members & Leaders:** Rotaractors who use the platform to check announcements, manage RSVPs, download official resources, and respond to weekly pulse trackers.
- **Board Admin Users:** Club executives managing announcements, initiatives, and page sections.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the premium, official digital home of the Rotaract Club of Vishwahita. It showcases Chennai-based initiatives with high-fidelity creative depth, drives member collaboration inside the Hub, and encourages community sponsorships and new member sign-ups.

## Brand Personality
- **Official & Professional:** Tailored to the visual identity and service standard of Rotary International.
- **Innovative & Modern:** Sleek, midnight-toned editorial layouts that feel like a premium digital instrument rather than a generic brochure.
- **Narrative-Driven:** A live, storytelling journey that guides visitors through the club's pulse, legacy, and direct impact.

## Anti-references
- **Flat Layouts:** Flat, solid-colored sections with standard borders and zero depth.
- **Generic Non-Profit Templates:** Bland, white-background templates with clip-art style emojis and uninspired grids.
- **Over-stimulating Gamification:** Overly bright, distracting animations or playful elements that clash with the official Rotaract credibility.

## Design Principles
- **Narrative Navigation:** The homepage layout acts as a coherent story, starting with the opening shot (Hero), proving the legacy (Timeline), defining the culture (Who We Are), showcasing execution (Bento Grid), revealing upcoming action (Calendar), displaying visual proofs (Gallery), and offering paths to join (Membership/Join Mission).
- **Midnight Cranberry & Gold Harmony:** Deep obsidian backdrops styled with the official Rotaract Cranberry (`#D41367`) and Rotary Gold (`#D4AF37`) to project prestige and official alignment.
- **Living Operations:** Real-time presence indicators, live project feeds, and active calendars to prove the club is active and functional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **WCAG AA Compliance:** High-contrast text elements, readable monospace data tables, and distinct focus indicators.
- **Prefers-Reduced-Motion:** Respecting user settings for motion transitions in GSAP scroll triggers and Framer Motion components.
