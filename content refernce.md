ABOUT SECTION
H2: A Legacy Built Since 1999
The Rotaract Club of Vishwahita was chartered on March 10, 1999, under the sponsorship of the Rotary Club of Madras Industrial City. For 27 years, the club has operated within District 3234 as a youth wing built on leadership, service, and measurable community impact.
The name Vishwahita means "universal friendship." It is not a tagline — it is the operating principle behind every project the club has run since its founding.
